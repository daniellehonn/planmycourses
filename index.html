How to use the planner

1. Make your course list

Open the template, then File → Make a copy. One row per course; put your most important courses near the top.

2. Fill in the columns

Course Number
e.g. CS 31. Other columns refer to courses by this name, spelled exactly the same.
Taken
Not Assigned, or the term you took it, like Fall, Year 1. That term is locked so Auto Plan won't add to it.
Planned
Optional. Export fills it in so a saved plan loads back the way you left it.
Req Satisfied
The requirement it counts toward, e.g. Major Core, GE. Separate several with commas.
Prerequisites
Courses needed first, separated by commas. Use / for either-or: CS 31 / CS 31E.
Corequisites
Courses taken earlier or in the same term.
Units, Difficulty
Units per course; difficulty (optional) is used to balance workload.

Leave out courses you've already satisfied elsewhere (AP, IB, transfer credit), and don't list them as prerequisites.

3. Load it

Download the sheet as CSV (File → Download → Comma-separated values) and upload it, or publish it (File → Share → Publish to web, choose your tab and Comma-separated values) and paste the link.

4. Plan

  • Auto Plan fills your terms. Change the timeline or max units first if you need to.
  • Drag a course to move it, or click it to see details and move it from there.
  • Pin a course to keep it where it is; lock a term to keep the whole term.
  • Courses turn red when a prerequisite isn't scheduled before them.

More settings

Workload

Balanced aims for an even load each term. Fastest fills terms up to the max (plus 4 units) to finish sooner.

Planner

Optimal solves the whole plan at once and usually fits more courses (about a second). Quick fills one term at a time and is instant. If Optimal can't load, Quick is used automatically.

Category priority

Drag to reorder. When courses compete for the same term, those in higher categories go first. Required courses are always placed before optional ones.

  • Load course data to see categories.

Graduation requirements

How many courses or units each category needs. Leave blank to plan every course you've listed. Auto Plan stops adding courses to a category once it's met.
